The maths is yours

The game gives you a market report, your own profit and loss, your balance sheet and what your rivals did last round. It does not give you the answer. There is a calculator and a notepad in the top bar, and the people who use them tend to win.

Some things never show up in a headline: buyer groups slowly grow or shrink, buyers are more price-sensitive in one game than the next, and experience makes your production cheaper over time. It is all in the numbers for whoever looks.

How you win

The company worth the most at the end wins. Company value is cash minus debt, plus factory, stores, stock, brand, earnings power and the shares you hold in rivals. There is one shortcut: take control of every other company and the game ends on the spot. That is a monopoly.

Three industries

Each industry has its own costs, buyers and news stories. Chocolate is the classic consumer market and the best place to start. In AI you sell subscriptions and run servers in a faster-growing market, with its own set of news stories.

Questions people ask

Is this a serious simulation or a party game?

Both, on purpose. The market model is a real one: price, quality, brand and availability decide who sells what, and nobody tells you how much you will sell. The wrapping is a party game with news broadcasts, a results show and takeovers.

How many people can play?

Up to 8 players in one game. If you are short of people, the host can add up to 4 computer-run companies, and you can play alone against bots.

Is Friendly Takeovers free?

Playing solo against bots is free, and so is your first online game with friends. After that the full game is a one-time purchase of €8.99, with unlimited online games. There is no subscription and there are no in-game purchases.

Do we need to download or install anything?

No. The game runs in the browser on a laptop, tablet or phone. One person creates a game and shares a 6-character code or an invite link; everyone else opens the link.

Can I play alone?

Yes. Solo against bots is free, with easy, normal and hard bots, and there is a daily challenge where everyone gets the same market and rivals and competes on one leaderboard.
