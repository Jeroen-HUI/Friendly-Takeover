Why it suits a voice call

It is loud in the right way. Rounds are secret until they resolve, then everyone sees the results at once. Someone always undercut someone.

Trades are public. When the stock exchange opens in round 3, every share purchase shows on a live ticker. Buying 10% of a friend's company while they are talking is a move.

Private deals are private. There are one-on-one chats inside the game for the deals you do not want the rest of the call to hear. The game enforces them when the round ends.

Short rounds. The host sets the timer. Blitz is five rounds of two and a half minutes.

Start one in a minute

Open the game, choose Multiplayer, create a game and paste the invite link in your channel. Add bots if you want a fuller market. When everyone has joined, press start.

Streaming it to the channel? There is a party-mode screen made for that: it shows the skyline, the timer, who is ready, the ticker and the results show.

We also run our own Discord for finding people to play with. The link is on the game's main menu.

Questions people ask

Is it a Discord bot or activity?

No. It is a browser game. You share the invite link in a channel or DM and everyone opens it in their browser, so it works in any server without permissions or bots.

How many people can play?

Up to 8 players in one game. If you are short of people, the host can add up to 4 computer-run companies, and you can play alone against bots.

Is Friendly Takeovers free?

Playing solo against bots is free, and so is your first online game with friends. After that the full game is a one-time purchase of €8.99, with unlimited online games. There is no subscription and there are no in-game purchases.

Does everyone need the same device?

No. Phones, tablets and laptops can all join the same game.
