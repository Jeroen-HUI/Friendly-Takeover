How a game night works

One person opens the game, picks Multiplayer and creates a game. They get a 6-character code and an invite link to drop in the group chat. Everyone who opens the link gets a company: same starting cash, same factory, same market.

Then you play in rounds. Each round everybody secretly sets a price, decides how much to produce, and spends on quality, marketing and research. When the timer runs out, all the decisions are resolved together and the results show plays: who sold out, who is stuck with a full warehouse, whose company is suddenly worth the most.

Why it works with a group

Everyone is in the same market. If your friend drops their price, your customers walk over to them. Nobody plays their own little game on the side.

You can buy each other. From round 3 a stock exchange opens. Buy enough shares in a friend's company and you take control of it. They can see you coming on the ticker, which is where the shouting usually starts.

Deals are real. In the chat you can offer shares, loans and non-attack pacts, to the whole table or in private. The game enforces whatever you agree, and if someone breaks a pact, the news calls it a betrayal in front of everyone.

There is a secret mission. Each player gets a private goal worth a bonus on the final score, so nobody is quite sure what the others are up to until the last screen.

How long does it take?

The host chooses the number of rounds and the time per round. The Blitz preset is five rounds of two and a half minutes, which fits in a lunch break. A standard game is eight rounds, and the host can set anything from 3 rounds to an open-ended game.

Questions people ask

Do we need to download or install anything?

No. The game runs in the browser on a laptop, tablet or phone. One person creates a game and shares a 6-character code or an invite link; everyone else opens the link.

How many people can play?

Up to 8 players in one game. If you are short of people, the host can add up to 4 computer-run companies, and you can play alone against bots.

Is Friendly Takeovers free?

Playing solo against bots is free, and so is your first online game with friends. After that the full game is a one-time purchase of €8.99, with unlimited online games. There is no subscription and there are no in-game purchases.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Everyone can play on their own phone. There is also a party mode: put the TV link on a big screen and it shows the skyline, the round timer, the news and the results show while everyone plays on their phone.

Can we play if we are not in the same room?

Yes. Games are online, with a built-in chat for the whole table and for private one-on-one deals, so it works over a voice call just as well as on the couch.
