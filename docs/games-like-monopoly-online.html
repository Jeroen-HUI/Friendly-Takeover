You can still win by monopoly

The normal way to win is to own the most valuable company when the last round ends. The other way is to take control of every rival company. The moment you do, the game is over and you have a monopoly.

Made for the table talk

The best part of any money game is the negotiating. Friendly Takeovers has a chat for the whole table and private chats per player, and deals you can actually sign: shares for cash, a loan that is repaid automatically a few rounds later, or a pact not to attack each other. Break a pact and you pay the penalty, and the news announces the betrayal to the whole table.

Questions people ask

Is this an official Monopoly game?

No. Friendly Takeovers is an independent game and is not affiliated with Hasbro or the Monopoly brand. It is a different game for people who like the same things: money, deals and takeovers.

How many people can play?

Up to 8 players in one game. If you are short of people, the host can add up to 4 computer-run companies, and you can play alone against bots.

Do we need to download or install anything?

No. The game runs in the browser on a laptop, tablet or phone. One person creates a game and shares a 6-character code or an invite link; everyone else opens the link.

Is Friendly Takeovers free?

Playing solo against bots is free, and so is your first online game with friends. After that the full game is a one-time purchase of €8.99, with unlimited online games. There is no subscription and there are no in-game purchases.
